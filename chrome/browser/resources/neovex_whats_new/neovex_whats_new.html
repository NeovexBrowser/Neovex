New Feature

Introducing Neovex Shield

Take back control of your privacy. Our completely rebuilt, native subresource tracking engine stops ads, trackers, and fingerprinting dead in their tracks.

Native C++ Interception

Unlike extensions, Shield operates natively within the Chromium engine, intercepting tracking scripts at the network layer before they even reach the browser renderer.

Real-Time Analytics

The New Tab Page widget now hooks directly into the browser process, providing live, to-the-second updates on every ad, tracker, and fingerprint attempt blocked.

Zero-Overhead Blocking

Because it's integrated natively, Neovex Shield requires zero extra memory and CPU overhead compared to bloated Javascript-based ad blocking extensions.

Anti-Fingerprinting

Automatically terminates subresource requests to domains known for executing complex canvas, font, and hardware fingerprinting techniques.
